# Course page template

Copy this for each course. Write the first paragraph for someone who does not work in your field.

---

# Course title

**In one sentence:** what a learner can do after this course that they could not before.

| | |
|---|---|
| Audience | Who it is for, and what they already know |
| Goal | One measurable outcome |
| Length | Hours or days, and how it is split |
| Format | Live, drop-in, self-paced, or mixed |
| Trainer | Who teaches it |

## Outline

A numbered list of sessions or lessons, each with a time and the activity (explain, demonstrate, practise, check).

## Materials

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Lesson plan (one page) | Timings, activities, what to prepare |
| Slides with speaker notes | The teaching deck |
| Learner handout | What the learner keeps |
| Assignment and answer key | Marked, hands-on work |
| Quiz and answer key | Ten questions with feedback |
| Rubric | How the trainer scores and gives feedback |
| Survey | What learners thought, and what changed because of it |
| Certificate | Given on completion |

## How learning was checked

The assignment mark, the quiz score, what a trainer observed during practice, and the follow-up (a week or a month later, could they still do it?).

## Results

Real numbers if they exist (completion, average score, time to first independent task); otherwise say plainly that the figures are sample data.

## What I would improve

Two or three honest changes for the next run.
