# Training portfolio

Courses, lesson plans, assignments and presentations I have designed and taught, and how I checked that people actually learned. I am a QA analyst with a background in customer support and training, and I like turning "how it works" into something a new person can follow on day one.

All companies, systems and people in these materials are invented. They are built the way real training is built: an audience, a goal, a lesson plan, hands-on work that is marked, and feedback that changes the next session.

## What a course pack contains

Each course is a page (what, who, how long, what was learned) plus the files a trainer actually uses: a one-page lesson plan, slides with speaker notes, a learner handout, a marked assignment with answer key, a short quiz, a rubric, a feedback survey and a certificate. The layout is in the [course page template](templates/course-page-template.md).
