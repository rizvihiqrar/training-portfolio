# Quiz

Six questions, given after session 3. One point each. Five or more means you are ready to work on a real project. Fewer than five means going back over the sessions in the [full version](full/jira-workflow-guide-full.md#the-three-sessions) before you do. The course page has the same quiz one question at a time, with the right answer and a reason after each pick.

## Questions

1. A fix is ready and the ticket is In Test. Whose turn is it?
2. Which part of a bug ticket do testers most often leave out?
3. A retest still fails. What do you do?
4. Why attach evidence?
5. What does this query find? `project = HBL AND status = "In Test"`
6. You moved a ticket to Done, then found a new bug nearby. What now?
