# About This Portfolio

How the courses are built, what is in each one, and how I check that people learned. Every company, system and person in these materials is made up, so nothing here comes from an employer.

## Overview

Training is how a new person becomes useful quickly. A good course tells you who it is for and what you will be able to do at the end. It shows how the trainer teaches it, how you check that people understood, and what to change next time. This portfolio shows all of those parts, not only the slides.

## At a Glance

- 1 course so far: a two-week plan that teaches testers to use automation tools, one hour a day (more on the way, see [coming next](#coming-next))
- Every course has a page, a one-page lesson plan, a quiz with answers, a scoring guide and a feedback form for learners
- Four Word files you can download and use: the schedule, a ten-day cheat sheet, a guide to the kinds of testing, and a test plan template
- Two templates ([course page](templates/course-page-template.md) and [lesson plan](templates/lesson-plan-template.md)), so every course is laid out the same way
- Honest results: where there are no learner numbers, the page says so
- A [check](https://github.com/rizvihiqrar/training-portfolio/blob/main/.github/scripts/check-readme.mjs) that stops the front page from linking to a missing file or forgetting a new course

## What Is in a Course Pack

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Course page | Who it is for, the goal, the length, the outline, how learning was checked, results, and what I would improve |
| Lesson plan (one page) | Timings, activities, what to prepare, and what to cut if time runs short |
| Slides with speaker notes | The deck the trainer teaches from |
| Learner handout (job aid) | What the learner keeps |
| Assignment and answers | Marked, hands-on work |
| Quiz and answers | Ten questions with feedback |
| Scoring guide | How the trainer scores and gives feedback |
| Feedback form | What learners thought, and what changed because of it |
| Certificate | Given when the course is finished |

The first course has the page, lesson plan, quiz, scoring guide, feedback form and Word handouts. Slides, an assignment and a certificate come with later courses. The layout is in the [course page template](templates/course-page-template.md) and the [lesson plan template](templates/lesson-plan-template.md).

## How I Check That People Learned

- **During the lesson:** A quick check at the end of every step (a question, a short exercise, a show of hands), so someone who is lost is found in minutes, not weeks.
- **Halfway:** A midpoint check, so anyone who is behind gets help before the second half starts.
- **At the end:** A marked assignment, a quiz, and a practical round scored with the scoring guide.
- **Later:** One question a month on, "have you used this since?" A good score on the day shows people understood. It does not show that their work changed.
- **After each run:** The feedback and the common mistakes become the first change for the next run, written at the bottom of the course page.

## Words Used on This Page

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Onboarding | The first weeks of a new person: what they learn, and when |
| Lesson plan | A one-page plan for a session: steps, timings, checks |
| Scoring guide (rubric) | A guide that says what each score looks like, so two trainers mark the same way |
| Benchmark | A task a learner must do to show they have reached a stage |
| Practical round | A hands-on task at the end of a course, scored with a scoring guide |
| Job aid | A one-page guide a person keeps beside them while they work |
| Enablement | Training and materials that help a team do its work well |
| Go-live | The day a customer or team starts using a system for real work |
| Runbook | Step-by-step instructions for handling a known situation |
