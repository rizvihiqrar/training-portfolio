# About Me

I am Iqrar Rizvi, a QA analyst who also trains people and builds small product and design pieces.

I started in customer support, moved into training new hires and writing the help articles they used, and then into quality assurance, where my team and I automated our regression tests. Each step taught me the same lesson: people do their best work when someone has turned how a thing works into something they can use on their first day.

That is what I like building most: a lesson plan, a clear page, a test that tells you exactly what broke, a decision written down with the reasons behind it.

## What I Do

### Product and Design

- Sort a long list of ideas into what to build first, and say why
- Weigh trade-offs and say what I chose not to do
- Measure whether a decision worked, honestly
- Next: a public app teardown, clickable prototypes, requirements and metrics
- See the [product portfolio](https://rizvihiqrar.github.io/product-portfolio/)

### Quality and Risk Assurance

- Decide what to test first when time is short
- Automate regression tests so a release does not depend on a week of manual checks
- Tell the people who decide what was and was not covered
- Review AI-written work before it is trusted
- See the [QA automation platform](https://github.com/rizvihiqrar/qa-automation-platform)

### Training

- Design courses, lesson plans, quizzes and scoring guides
- Teach hands-on, so people practise instead of only watching
- Check that people learned, then improve the next run
- See the [training portfolio](https://rizvihiqrar.github.io/training-portfolio/)

## How I Work

I work in small pieces and show my reasoning, including what I chose not to do. Each case study uses real numbers from my own projects, labels a rule of thumb as one, and ends with what I would change.
